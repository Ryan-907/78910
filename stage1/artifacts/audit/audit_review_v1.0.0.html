Misses are the dangerous direction. A false positive (tagger reports a type that is absent) causes the future gate check to deny a flow it should permit — visible and self-correcting. A miss (type present, tagger silent) lets the subset test pass over information it never saw: the document is released, the recipient holds an information type they should not, and the decision looks correct. Please read for what is present and unmarked, not only for whether the highlighted spans are right.
Record your verdicts in audit_sheet_v1.0.0.csv, one row per (document, information type): put y or n in present_in_document. This page is the evidence view; the CSV is the answer sheet. Then run python -m stage1.score_audit.

RIVERSIDE MEDICAL CLINIC
INTERNAL POLICY MEMORANDUM

Date: January 5, 2026
To: All Clinical, Billing, HR, and IT Staff
From: Compliance Office
Re: Updated HIPAA Minimum Necessary Policy — Acknowledgment Required

Effective immediately, Riverside Medical Clinic has revised its HIPAA Minimum Necessary Use and Disclosure Policy (Policy No. HIPAA-MN-2026-01) in response to updated OCR guidance issued in Q4 2025.

Key Changes:
1. Staff must access only the patient information required to perform their specific job function. Role-based access controls will be enforced in the EHR system beginning February 1, 2026.
2. Verbal disclosures in shared workspaces must be limited to the minimum information necessary. Use of private consultation rooms is required when discussing protected health information (PHI).
3. All bulk data exports or report pulls involving PHI must receive prior approval from the Compliance Office.
4. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Required Action:
All staff must read the full updated policy (available on the internal portal under Compliance > HIPAA Policies) and submit a signed acknowledgment form to Human Resources no later than January 31, 2026.

Questions should be directed to the Compliance Office at ext. 4400.

Thank you for your continued commitment to patient privacy.

RIVERSIDE MEDICAL CLINIC
Notice of Privacy Practices
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

YOUR HEALTH INFORMATION RIGHTS

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can access this information. Please review it carefully.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
Riverside Medical Clinic uses your protected health information (PHI) for treatment, payment, and healthcare operations as permitted under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164.

YOUR RIGHTS INCLUDE:
- Requesting access to or copies of your medical records
- Requesting corrections to your health information
- Requesting restrictions on how we share your information
- Receiving a list of disclosures we have made
- Receiving this notice in alternative formats upon request

OUR RESPONSIBILITIES
We are required by law to maintain the privacy of your PHI, provide this notice, and follow its terms.

COMPLAINTS
If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with our Privacy Officer or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services at www.hhs.gov/ocr.

CONTACT US
Riverside Medical Clinic — Privacy Officer
400 Riverside Parkway, Suite 100
Phone: (800) 555-0100

No retaliation will occur for filing a complaint.
